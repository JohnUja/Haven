# Subscription Plans & Limits Summary

## Plans Overview

### Free Tier
- **Daily Tasks/Blocks:** 3/day
- **Active Routines:** 1
- **AI Insights:** 3/week
- **Premium Themes:** None
- **Price:** Free

### Haven+ (Recommended for Most Users)
- **Daily Tasks/Blocks:** Unlimited
- **Active Routines:** 5
- **AI Insights:** Unlimited
- **Premium Themes:** 5 included
- **Price:** $6.99/month or $59.99/year (Save 28%)

### Haven Pro (Best Value)
- **Daily Tasks/Blocks:** Unlimited
- **Active Routines:** 5
- **AI Insights:** Unlimited
- **Premium Themes:** All
- **Personalized AI:** ✅
- **Mood-to-Theme Sync:** ✅
- **Cloud Backups:** Unlimited
- **Offline AI Model:** ✅
- **Price:** $9.99/month or $79.99/year (Save 33%)

### Haven Forever (One-Time)
- **Everything in Haven Pro**
- **Lifetime access**
- **All future themes**
- **Priority support**
- **Price:** $120 one-time

## Recommendations

**For New Users:** Start Free → Upgrade to Haven+ if needed → Consider Haven Pro for AI features

**For Power Users:** Haven Pro (Yearly) - Best value at $6.66/month

**For Long-Term Users:** Haven Forever - Break-even in ~12 months
