# Haven 2.0 Subscription Plans & Limits

## Plan Overview

### Free Tier
**Price:** Free

**Limits:**
- **Daily Tasks/Blocks:** 3 per day
- **Active Routines:** 1 routine
- **AI Insights:** 3 per week
- **Premium Themes:** None (default theme only)
- **Personalized AI:** ❌
- **Mood-to-Theme Sync:** ❌
- **Cloud Backups:** ❌
- **Offline AI Model:** ❌

**Best For:** Users trying out the app, casual task management

---

### Haven+ (Recommended for Most Users)
**Price:** 
- Monthly: $6.99/month
- Yearly: $59.99/year (Save 28% - $4.99/month equivalent)

**Limits:**
- **Daily Tasks/Blocks:** Unlimited
- **Active Routines:** Up to 5 routines
- **AI Insights:** Unlimited
- **Premium Themes:** 5 premium themes included
- **Personalized AI:** ❌
- **Mood-to-Theme Sync:** ❌
- **Cloud Backups:** ❌
- **Offline AI Model:** ❌

**Best For:** Power users who want unlimited tasks, multiple routines, and AI insights without advanced AI features

**Why Recommended:** Best value for most users who need unlimited tasks and routines without the premium AI features.

---

### Haven Pro (Best Value)
**Price:**
- Monthly: $9.99/month
- Yearly: $79.99/year (Save 33% - $6.66/month equivalent)

**Limits:**
- **Daily Tasks/Blocks:** Unlimited
- **Active Routines:** Up to 5 routines
- **AI Insights:** Unlimited
- **Premium Themes:** All premium themes included
- **Personalized AI:** ✅ (AI learns your patterns and preferences)
- **Mood-to-Theme Sync:** ✅ (Automatic theme changes based on mood)
- **Cloud Backups:** Unlimited
- **Offline AI Model:** ✅ (AI works without internet)

**Best For:** Users who want the full experience with advanced AI features, theme customization, and offline capabilities

**Why Best Value:** Only $3/month more than Haven+ but includes all premium features, personalized AI, and lifetime theme access.

---

### Haven Forever (One-Time Purchase)
**Price:** $120 one-time payment

**Limits:**
- **Daily Tasks/Blocks:** Unlimited
- **Active Routines:** Up to 5 routines
- **AI Insights:** Unlimited
- **Premium Themes:** All current and future themes included
- **Personalized AI:** ✅
- **Mood-to-Theme Sync:** ✅
- **Cloud Backups:** Unlimited
- **Offline AI Model:** ✅
- **Lifetime Updates:** ✅
- **Priority Support:** ✅

**Best For:** Long-term users who want lifetime access and don't want recurring subscriptions

**Value Calculation:** 
- Break-even point: ~12 months vs Haven Pro yearly
- Break-even point: ~17 months vs Haven+ yearly
- Best for users planning to use the app for 2+ years

## Recommendations

### For New Users
1. **Start with Free** - Try the app with 3 tasks/day limit
2. **Upgrade to Haven+** if you need more than 3 tasks/day or want multiple routines
3. **Upgrade to Haven Pro** if you want AI features and theme customization

### For Power Users
**Recommended: Haven Pro (Yearly)**
- Best value at $6.66/month
- All features included
- Personalized AI for better productivity
- Theme customization

### For Long-Term Users
**Recommended: Haven Forever**
- One-time payment
- All current and future features
- Break-even in ~12 months
- Best for users committed to long-term use

---

## Upgrade Triggers

The paywall appears when users try to:
1. **Create 4th task/block** (Free tier limit: 3/day)
2. **Create 2nd routine** (Free tier limit: 1 routine)
3. **Use 4th AI insight** (Free tier limit: 3/week)

For logged-in users on Free tier, the paywall shows all available plans with clear feature comparison.
